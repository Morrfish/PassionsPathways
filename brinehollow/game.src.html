Issue #1 · The Tide and the Lantern
One lane of the harbor, 3 v 3. Kill the Rust crew's Foghorn, then cut their Anchor Chain to win. The tide turns every 70 seconds: at low tide the seabed trench on your left is a dry flank with Reef Crabs (Pearls); at high tide the street floods and the trench becomes a fast current. Each wave has a glowing Lantern-Bearer — kill it, grab the lantern and run it to one of your buoys for a big Spark payout and a bigger next wave.
