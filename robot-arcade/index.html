For the teacher

  • Everything runs in the browser with no logins. Prize shelves, mission stars and the last program are saved on each computer.
  • Each game has Run, Step (one line at a time, good for debugging) and a speed setting. The camera starts in front, and in Auto it switches to a top view while things move. Students can also pick Front or Top themselves.
  • Movement numbers can be decimals (right 1.5, trolley out 2.25, forward 0.5), up to 2 digits after the point. repeat still needs a whole number.
  • When code has a mistake, the line turns red and a plain-English message says what went wrong ("The hook is 2 levels above the crate. Lower 2 more").
  • Adding Code Claw prizes: open robot-arcade/js/prizes.js and add a line like { name: 'Rubber Chicken', emoji: '🐔', rarity: 'common', kind: 'funny', note: 'Squeaks when sad.' }, Rarities are common, uncommon, rare, epic and legendary. Rarer prizes show up less often and hide farther from the chute.
  • Crane and rover missions are listed at the top of js/crane.js and js/rover.js.
