# Taleborn: ChatGPT Creature Prompts

_Generated by `tools/build_roster.py`._

**212 prompts in all.**

Every creature gets **two battle sprites**: a **FRONT** sprite (facing you, used when it is the wild or enemy creature) and a **BACK** sprite (seen from behind, used when it is your creature). Each line also gets party icons.

**Prompt IDs:** every prompt has an ID such as `SAK-01-F`. The letters are the realm, the number is the line, and the last letter is **F** = front sheet, **B** = back sheet, **I** = party icons. In ChatGPT you can just say *"Make prompt SAK-01-F"*, or paste the prompt text yourself (more reliable).

**Order for each line:** F first, then B in the same chat (so ChatGPT matches the designs), then I.
