CHECKFIRE
Chess where every capture is a toy-blaster duel. Win the duel, take the square.
How to play

It's real chess — every normal rule (castling, en passant, promotion, check and checkmate) applies. The twist: a capture is never automatic. When a piece attacks, both toy fighters drop into a room and settle it with foam-dart blasters.

  • Attacker wins: the capture happens and the attacker moves in, keeping whatever health it had left.
  • Defender wins: the attacker is knocked out, the defender holds its square, and the turn passes.
  • Wounds stick. Health carries over between battles. Every piece recovers 8 HP each time its side makes a move. Promoted pawns come back at full health.
  • Regicide: if a failed attack leaves a king exposed, the enemy may attack the king directly. A king that loses its duel loses the game.
  • Overtime: after 75 seconds the room starts to collapse and both fighters take damage. If both fall at once, the defender holds.

Loadout & Armory: every duel pays credits (more for winning, more still for winning with health left; winning the game pays a big bonus). Spend them on blasters for each piece type — including the Crossbow, which Pawns, Knights, Bishops and Kings can all carry — and on attachments: Scope (zoom), Stock (steadier), Big Clip (more darts), Power I–III, and Chrome / Gold / Diamond finishes (each adds damage). Before each duel you pick that piece's blaster, attachments, finish, gadget and room.

Gadgets (F): Grappling Hook zips you to any wall or toy it hits. Jetpack blasts you upward. Propeller Hat — the best — lifts you, slows your fall and lets you steer anywhere in the air.

Rooms: Kid's Bedroom, Chess Park, Library Table, Diner Counter, School Gym, Toy Store, Greenhouse, Arcade, Attic, Beach Boardwalk, plus four neon arenas.
