Checkfire

Chess where every capture is a firefight. Win the duel, take the square.

How to play

It's real chess — every normal rule (castling, en passant, promotion, check and checkmate) applies. The twist: a capture is never automatic. When a piece attacks, both pieces drop into a first-person arena and fight it out.

  • Attacker wins: the capture happens and the attacker moves in, keeping whatever health it had left.
  • Defender wins: the attacker is destroyed, the defender holds its square, and the turn passes.
  • Wounds stick. Health carries over between battles. Every piece recovers 8 HP each time its side makes a move. Promoted pawns come back at full health.
  • Regicide: if a failed attack leaves a king exposed, the enemy may attack the king directly. A king that loses its duel loses the game.
  • Overtime: after 75 seconds the arena collapses and both fighters take damage. If both fall at once, the defender holds.
