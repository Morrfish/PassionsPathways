## Their 4 special moves

charge forward, fly up, teleport, slam down, spin, block-and-hit-back, heal, leave a trap, power up.

2. **Side special** (a rush or charge). Example: "shopping-cart charge"

4. **Down special** (anything: trap, heal, slam, counter). Example: "spills a LEGO trap"
