How to play

Knock everyone off the stage. Every hit raises a player's damage %. The higher it is, the farther they fly. Fly past the edge of the screen and you lose a life. Last one standing wins.

Attacks change with direction. Attack alone, or hold left/right, up or down while pressing it. It works on the ground and in the air.

Every fighter has 4 specials: Special alone, side + Special, up + Special (also gets you back to the stage), and down + Special.

Dodge to be untouchable for a moment. Hold a direction to roll or air-dodge that way. Press down on a floating platform to drop through it. Press down in the air to fall fast.
